Interior Buddy

Scan the house with the iPhone camera. Choose a style, or connect a Pinterest board you own, then walk the rooms in AR with a new layout locked to the floors you captured.

What it does

Interior Buddy is an iOS app. It stays on the phone. Scans, floor-plan photos, and generated layouts are stored on the device. If you connect Pinterest, you grant Interior Buddy permission to read your own public boards and pins so it can infer a style from that board. The app does not post Pins, edit boards, or use Pinterest ads.
